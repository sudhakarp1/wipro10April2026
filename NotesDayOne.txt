Bitwise operators:
------------------
	performing operations at bit-level of your integers.
	
	that is 
		print(10 & 8) --> 
			bitwise AND operation 
				10 --> 1010
				8  --> 1000 AND 
				------------
					   1000 --> 8 Ans 
				------------
				
		print(10 | 8) --> 
			bitwise OR operation 
				10 --> 1010
				8  --> 1000 OR 
				------------
					   1010 --> 10 Ans 
				------------
				
		print(10 ^ 8) --> 
			bitwise XOR operation 
				10 --> 1010
				8  --> 1000 XOR 
				------------
					   0010 --> 2 Ans 
				------------
